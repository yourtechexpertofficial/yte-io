About — the ecosystem

One person, turning ideas into products.

Cosvire is not a team, a fund, or a roadmap. It is the structure I built so my own ideas actually get finished.

01 — Why it exists
01The problem

Ideas kept dying in notebooks

The bottleneck was never coming up with things worth building. It was that every idea started from zero — new decisions about design, structure and tooling, every single time.

Cosvire is the fix. One set of standards, one design language, one way of working, reused by everything I make.

02The first result

YTE Icons

The first product to come out of the ecosystem — a custom icon set of 35 glyphs, shipped as a webfont in four formats.

It proved the approach works: an idea went in one end and came out the other as something real that gets used.

03Where it stands

Early, and honest about it

One product released, more being built. I would rather show one real thing than a page of names for products that do not exist yet.

Everything new gets added here once it is genuinely usable.

02 — How I work

Six rules I actually follow.

01

Simplicity first

The best solution is the simplest one that fully solves the problem. Anything that does not need to exist gets removed.

02

Ship before perfect

Something real in the world beats something perfect on a whiteboard. Release, learn from actual use, improve.

03

Own the craft

Every pixel and every function is intentional. Nothing ships just because it was easier to leave alone.

04

Build on what exists

Each product reuses the standards the last one established, so nothing starts from zero twice.

05

Say what is true

Only finished, usable things get presented as products. No vapourware, no fake roadmaps.

06

Build in public

The work, the reasoning and the mistakes are all visible. That is what makes it worth following.

COSVIRE.

An ecosystem built to bring ideas to life. Independent, and built in public by one person.
