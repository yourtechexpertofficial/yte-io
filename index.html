02 — The method

Three stages. No committee.

Everything goes through the same three stages — or it stops early, which is just as useful an outcome.

Stage I

Shape it

A sentence becomes a spec. The idea gets argued with until it's either something buildable or a written reason not to bother. Most die here. That's the job.

Stage II

Build it

Spec becomes software, built on whatever the last thing established — so the design language and the standards never get reinvented from scratch.

Stage III

Release it

It goes out, gets used, gets fixed. Nothing here is ever finished — it's shipped, and then it gets better.
