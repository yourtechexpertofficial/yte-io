02 — Process

How an idea becomes a product.

The ecosystem is a way of working, not a slogan. Every idea goes through the same three stages — or it stops, which is just as useful.

Stage I

Shape it

A sentence becomes a specification. The idea gets argued with until it is either something buildable or a written reason not to build it. Most ideas stop here, and that is the point.

Stage II

Build it

The specification becomes software, engineered against whatever the previous products already established — so the design language and standards do not get reinvented each time.

Stage III

Release it

It goes out to people, gets used, and gets revised. Nothing here is ever finished — it is released, and then improved.
