Cookie policy

Current version

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website, allowing the site to remember information about your visit.

2. How we use them

  • Essential — required for the site to function; these cannot be disabled
  • Analytics — help us understand how visitors use the site
  • Preference — remember your settings between visits

3. Third parties

A small number of trusted third-party services may set their own cookies, contractually bound to handle data consistently with this policy.

4. Managing cookies

Cookies can be refused or deleted through your browser settings. Disabling some may affect functionality.
