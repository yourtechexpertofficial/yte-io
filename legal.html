Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit, making the next visit easier and the site more useful.

Cosvire uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function correctly. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our site so we can improve it.
